## Visual corrections
Extra Small, Small, Medium and Large now scale headline, description, eyebrow, content padding, action spacing and CTA text/button dimensions. Media and slide frames remain uniform per selection. Extra Small uses a markedly smaller headline and tighter padding; medium remains the normal default. Remove persistent reserved scroll-gutter; permit genuine long-content scrolling rather than clipping it. Separate mobile typography sizes maintain readability and support narrow screens. Slide text and images are unchanged.

## Editorial corrections
Remove redundant header tagline, footer QR exhortation, duplicate homepage kitchen navigation/copy, superfluous card section eyebrows, overly verbose demonstration menu introduction, redundant shop hero subtitle, repeated cart/account introductions, and redundant contact/FAQ/checkout instructions. Condense operational hints in Homepage Carousel Admin. Do not change user-authored slide content, product listings, status, price or fulfillment settings.

## Protected content
Preserve legitimate catalogue/demo not-for-sale labels, pickup/payment terms, food and allergen guidance, cottage-food and refund policies, special-order quote acceptance, checkout address requirements, contact consent, account permissions, and safety notices. These are not discretionary filler.

GitHub CI and Cloudflare production revision checks; browser review of all four presets on desktop/mobile with typical and long headings, navigation accessibility and customer workflows. Automated tests alone cannot prove absence of overflow in every browser.
